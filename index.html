Fix quadratic BPE merging for long pieces

The tokenizer's byte-pair merge degraded to O(mn) on long pieces. Replaced it with a binary heap over linked boundary arrays for large inputs, cutting merge work to O(m log n) while keeping the fast path for short pieces.
